Integrated Pest Management

Don't kill the "Good Bugs." IPM uses nature to fight nature, reducing the need for toxic sprays.

  • Biological Control: Let ladybugs eat the aphids.
  • Trap Cropping: Lure pests away from your main profit.

Natural Treatment Lab

Neem Oil Spray

Best for: Aphids & Mites

Recipe: 5ml cold-pressed Neem oil + 2ml liquid soap + 1L water. Apply every 7 days.

Garlic Repellent

Best for: Natural Pest Deterrent

Recipe: Blend 1 bulb garlic with 1L water. Sit overnight, strain, dilute 1:10.

Soda Fungicide

Best for: Mildew & Spots

Recipe: 1 tbsp baking soda + 1 tsp veg oil + 1 gallon water. Spray on leaves.
